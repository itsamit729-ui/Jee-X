' $x$ ' is the product which is obtained from propanenitrile and stannous chloride in the presence of hydrochloric acid followed by hydrolysis. ' $y$ ' is the product which is obtained from the but-2-ene by the ozonolysis followed by hydrolysis. From the following, which product is not obtained when one mole of ' $x$ ' and one mole of ' $y$ ' react with each other in the presence of alkali followed by heating?
A2-Methylpent-2-enal
B2-Methylbut-2-enal
C3-Methylbut-2-enal
DPent-2-enal

Solution

Key concepts: the Stephen reduction turns a nitrile into an aldehyde. Ozonolysis splits a C=C bond into two carbonyl compounds. Two different aldehydes that both have α‑H give a crossed aldol condensation, which forms four products.
Step 1 – Find $x$ (Stephen reduction): $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2C{\equiv}N \xrightarrow{SnCl_2/HCl} CH_3CH_2CH{=}NH\cdot HCl \xrightarrow{H_2O} CH_3CH_2CHO}$. So $x$ = propanal.
Step 2 – Find $y$ (ozonolysis): $\mathrm{CH_3CH{=}CHCH_3 \xrightarrow{O_3,\ Zn/H_2O} 2\,CH_3CHO}$. So $y$ = ethanal.
Step 3 – Both aldehydes have α‑H. With dilute alkali and heat they give a crossed aldol condensation, which produces four α,β‑unsaturated aldehydes.
Step 4 – Propanal + propanal (self condensation): $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2CH{=}C(CH_3)CHO}$ = 2‑methylpent‑2‑enal (A).
Step 5 – Ethanal + ethanal (self condensation): $\mathrm{CH_3CH{=}CHCHO}$ = but‑2‑enal.
Step 6 – The carbanion from ethanal attacks the C=O of propanal: $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2CH{=}CHCHO}$ = pent‑2‑enal (D).
Step 7 – The carbanion from propanal attacks the C=O of ethanal: $\mathrm{CH_3CH{=}C(CH_3)CHO}$ = 2‑methylbut‑2‑enal (B).
Step 8 – 3‑Methylbut‑2‑enal is $\mathrm{(CH_3)_2C{=}CHCHO}$. Making it would need acetone as the carbonyl partner, and acetone is not present. So this product cannot form.
Answer: (C) 3‑Methylbut‑2‑enal

Consider the following two reactions A and B . Numerical value of [molar mass of $x+$ molar mass of $y$ ] is $\text{\_\_\_\_}$

B160
C88
D4

Solution

**Reaction A:** phenol is acidic, so it reacts with the active metal sodium and releases hydrogen gas.
$$2\,\mathrm{C_6H_5OH} + 2\,\mathrm{Na} \rightarrow 2\,\mathrm{C_6H_5ONa} + \mathrm{H_2}\uparrow$$
So gas $x = \mathrm{H_2}$, with molar mass $2\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$.
**Reaction B:** benzoic acid is a stronger acid than carbonic acid, so it reacts with $\mathrm{NaHCO_3}$ and releases carbon dioxide (effervescence).

AB
BD
CA
DC

Solution

Tollens' reagent $\mathrm{[Ag(NH_3)_2]^+}$ oxidises aldehydes. So the compound must have a free $\mathrm{-CHO}$ group, or be able to form one in solution.
Compound A (2-methoxytetrahydrofuran) is a cyclic acetal. Acetals do not open in the basic Tollens' medium, so A gives no free aldehyde and the test is negative.

A$\;\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}+x^{2}} \times p$
B$\;\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}-x^{2}} \times p$

Solution
